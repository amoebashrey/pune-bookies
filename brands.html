A few honest notes.

Pune Bookies is a reading community in Pune. We meet every Sunday under a tree. About 250 of us, give or take, for two hours, in silence. It's been happening every Sunday since early 2024.

We're not a publication. We're not an event series. We're not building toward a platform. The Sunday is the thing — not a funnel to anything else. We don't charge for it, we don't take emails at the door, and we don't put anyone's branding on the tree.

And still: we like working with brands. Some of our favourite Sundays had a partner on the grass. The ones that work are the ones where the brand brings something that belongs there — a thing readers are quietly glad to find next to them. Not signage. Not a campaign. Something a reader would keep.

a short list

Who we're a fit for.

  • Books, publishing, and the people who sell them
  • Audio — audiobooks, podcasts, things for ears
  • Coffee, chai, and breakfast-shaped food
  • Stationery, paper, pens that people get attached to
  • Lifestyle brands with a reading soul, not a reading campaign

Not on the list but sure you belong on the grass? Write anyway. The list has been wrong before.

so there are no surprises later

Things we won't do, even for the right brand.

  1. Charge readers to attend.
  2. Add your branding to the tree, the WhatsApp, or the Sunday itself.
  3. Capture emails on your behalf.
  4. Manufacture “moments” for content.
  5. Pretend a Sunday was about you.
