A few honest notes.

Pune Bookies is a reading community in Pune. We meet every Sunday under a tree. About 250 of us, give or take, for two hours, in silence. It’s been happening every Sunday for almost two years.

We’re not a publication. We’re not an event series. We’re not building toward a platform. The Sunday is the thing — not a funnel to anything else. We don’t charge for it, we don’t take emails at the door, and we don’t put anyone’s branding on the tree.

We talk to brands sometimes. The conversations that work are the ones where the brand wants to contribute something that belongs on a Sunday — a thing readers would actually be glad to find on the grass next to them. Not signage. Not a campaign. Something quieter.

Things we won’t do, even for the right brand

  1. Charge readers to attend.
  2. Add your branding to the tree, the WhatsApp, or the Sunday itself.
  3. Capture emails on your behalf.
  4. Manufacture “moments” for content.
  5. Pretend a Sunday was about you.

If you’d like to talk

We don’t have a pitch deck. We have a tree, a Sunday, and an opinion about what belongs on it. If you’d like to explore something together, write to hello@punebookies.com with one paragraph about what you have in mind. We reply slowly. We mean that as a compliment to your patience.
